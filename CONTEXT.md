# Hollow campaign language

**Keeper**: An allied combatant carrying the valley's lantern oath.
**Round**: One allied phase followed by one enemy phase.
**Action**: An attack, heal, guard, wait, or beacon lighting that exhausts a Keeper.
**Bond**: Persistent trust between two Keepers, strengthened by adjacent combat support.
**Support**: Attack and defense assistance from an adjacent living ally or a paired reserve.
**Chapter checkpoint**: The army at a battle's start, restored on retry.
**Fallen**: Unable to act for the rest of this battle; recovered at camp.

**Pair**: Two living Keepers sharing one square, with a front that receives orders and a reserve that may assist.

**Dual Strike**: A chance-based partner follow-up during a front unit’s attack or counter.

**Dual Guard**: A chance-based partner interception that prevents one blow against the front.
